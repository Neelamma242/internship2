# internship2
overview

WaveCon, a telecom operator, launched 5G and wanted to know whether it grew the business. This project compares 4 months before launch (Jan to Apr 2022) with 4 months after launch (Jun to Sep 2022) across overall revenue, customer KPIs, and the plan portfolio, then turns the findings into recommendations for the leadership team.

Headline: 5G moved the mix, not the top line. Revenue held flat, but ARPU rose while active users fell and churn increased.

Business questions answered
What was the revenue impact of the 5G launch?
Which KPI is underperforming the most?
Which plans are winning and which are losing?
Which plan is most affected by the move to 5G?
Were any plans discontinued, and what does that signal?
Key findings
Metric	Before 5G	After 5G	Change
Total revenue	Rs 1,597.7 Cr	Rs 1,589.7 Cr	-0.5%
ARPU	Rs 190 / user	Rs 211 / user	+11.1%
Active users	843.5 L	773.7 L	-8.3%
Unsubscribed (churn)	56.3 L	69.6 L	+23.5%
Revenue is flat, the mix is not. Higher-value 5G-ready plans (p11 to p13) generated Rs 333.5 Cr in four months, covering losses from legacy plans.
Churn is the weakest KPI. It rose in 14 of 15 cities (Mumbai was the exception), and the active-user decline mirrors it.
Winners: p11 Ultra Fast Mega (+Rs 185.95 Cr), p12 Ultra Duo Data (+Rs 116.13 Cr), p1 Smart Recharge Pack (+Rs 57.39 Cr).
Most affected plan: p7, the 25 GB Combo 3G/4G Pack, fell from Rs 58.24 Cr to Rs 15.56 Cr (-73.3%).
Discontinued at launch: Daily Saviour (Rs 43.43 Cr before), Combo TopUp (Rs 22.68 Cr), Big Combo Pack (Rs 13.11 Cr).
Recommendations
Focus on retention. Find out why customers leave, starting with the cities where churn rose most.
Accelerate portfolio migration. Retire or re-platform p7 and move its subscribers to 5G-ready plans such as p11.
Double down on proven 5G plans. Understand what drives p11 and p12 and replicate it in other segments.
Check migration of discontinued plans. Confirm whether their customers moved to new plans or churned.

Methodology
Comparison windows: Jan to Apr 2022 (before) vs. Jun to Sep 2022 (after), aligned by month-of-cycle.
ARPU: average revenue per user.
Churn: unsubscribed users per period.
Plan impact: change in revenue per plan between the two windows. New plans have no "before" revenue, and discontinued plans have no "after" revenue.
